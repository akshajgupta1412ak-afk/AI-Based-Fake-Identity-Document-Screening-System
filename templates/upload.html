Pre-Screening Image Quality Assessment Before performing fraud screening, the system automatically checks:
  • Resolution (adequate optical dimensions)
  • Blur (Laplacian focus variance)
  • Brightness (underexposure / overexposure)
  • Orientation (aspect ratio consistency)
  • Text visibility (edge contrast)
If image quality is insufficient, you will be prompted to upload a clearer image. (Poor image quality is not classified as suspicious).
