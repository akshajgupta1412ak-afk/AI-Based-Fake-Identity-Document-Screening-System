Project blueprint

DocSec AI is an explainable first-line triage tool for verification officers in KYC, admissions, employment checks and public services. It screens identity documents for digital manipulation, expiry and synthetic generation.

  • Explainable: every risk penalty links to visual or syntactic evidence.
  • Integrity: SHA-256 hashing catches duplicate and altered re-uploads.
  • Dual forensics: Error Level Analysis plus Fourier noise profiling.
  • Human in the loop: a calibrated score and three decision tiers.

Supports Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, passport and voter ID. Built with Python 3.13, Flask 3.1, OpenCV 4.10, PyMuPDF and SQLite.

Editing tools and generative AI make document fraud cheap, and remote onboarding relies on uploaded scans. Manual review misses micro-splicing, plain OCR checks syntax but not pixels, and black-box models can’t justify a decision in an audit.

  • Fatigue: reviewers miss altered fonts and splices at volume.
  • Shallow OCR: blind to recompression and pasted patches.
  • Opaque models: no evidence for appeals.
  • Non-ID uploads: wasted checks on photos and blank pages.
  • Extraction and syntax: read PDF, JPG and PNG, then validate fields and date order.
  • Tampering forensics: ELA at 90% recompression plus contour detection.
  • Generative screening: noise variance and typography baselines.
  • Cryptographic integrity: SHA-256 on every upload.
  • Risk score: eight checkpoints combined into three tiers.
  • Hardening: PBKDF2-SHA256 passwords, parameterised SQL, 5 MB limit, HTTPOnly sessions.
  • Ingestion and fingerprinting of the uploaded file.
  • Normalisation to a standard canvas.
  • Image quality pre-check.
  • Gate: is this a valid identity document? If not, reject early.
  • OCR and syntax verification.
  • OpenCV tampering forensics and synthetic indicators, in parallel.
  • Local registry cross-check.
  • Risk calibration on the 0–100 scale.
  • Three-tier triage with an action protocol.
  • Audit dossier and PDF report.
