Core Capabilities

Engineered for Explainable Triage

Deterministic optical extraction, pixel-level tampering heuristics, and calibrated risk tiers designed for KYC officers and verification analysts.

Optical Field Parsing

Multi-engine OCR with Aadhaar, PAN, Passport and Driving Licence regex validation. Cross-checks chronological date consistency.

Dual-stage Error Level Analysis (ELA) at 90% recompression plus contour edge detection to detect spliced text and pasted photo patches.

Inspection Pipeline

How A Document Is Checked

Every uploaded file moves through eight defense-in-depth checkpoints to produce an auditable score.

01

Ingest & Hash

Cryptographic SHA-256 byte fingerprinting logs submissions and identifies altered re-uploads.

02

Quality Pre-Gate

Resolution, focus sharpness and exposure checks ensure the document is clear enough for OCR.

03

Dual Forensics

OpenCV ELA tampering heuristics and generative synthetic noise metrics run in parallel.

04

Risk Calibration

A calibrated 0–100 score categorizes the document into Likely Genuine, Verification, or Suspicious.

Engineered for Academic & Industry Rigor

DocSec AI is developed as an explainable triage framework for KYC officers, academic admissions, and public services. Every risk penalty maps to concrete pixel or syntactic evidence, providing human reviewers with complete audit defensibility.
